# SENTINEL — Autonomous Fleet Readiness

## Overview
**SENTINEL** is an operational sustainment and readiness command platform engineered for defense technology operations. It continuously monitors, triages, and optimizes the operational availability of 50 deployed autonomous ground vehicles (`MRD-001` through `MRD-050`).

The primary question SENTINEL answers for command staff and maintenance operations is:
> **“How much of the fleet is available right now, what is reducing readiness, and what should operations fix first?”**

---

## The Problem
Operating autonomous ground fleets across distributed forward operating bases and testing proving grounds creates significant operational friction when telemetry, maintenance scheduling, firmware configurations, and spare parts supply chains remain siloed. When unscheduled faults occur, lack of correlation obscures whether downtime is caused by depot bay bottlenecks, supply chain part stockouts, or firmware release anomalies.

---

## The SENTINEL Solution
SENTINEL provides a unified, real-time command dashboard with 7 core modules:

1. **Fleet Command Dashboard**: Real-time 82% fleet availability KPI, status breakdown visualizer, comparative readiness matrixes (by Location, Unit, Hardware Generation, and Software Version), and immediate Top Readiness Drivers.
2. **50-Asset Inventory**: Full visibility over assets `MRD-001` to `MRD-050`, with multi-dimensional filtering, searching, sorting, and fast triage.
3. **Asset Telemetry & Detail View**: Deep subsystem health indicators (battery core, powertrain, avionics, comms), installed LRUs, fault history, work order history, 200-hour inspection certifications, and event timelines.
4. **Maintenance & Depot Bay Operations**: Complete work order lifecycles (Open, In Progress, Awaiting Parts, Quality Inspection, Completed) with technician allocation and parts reservation.
5. **Fault Management & Diagnostic Triage**: Anomaly tracking classified by severity (Critical, Moderate, Low, Advisory) and subsystem impact.
6. **Configuration Management & Telemetry Correlations**: Cross-matrix distribution (Gen 2, Gen 2.5, Gen 3 × v4.6.1, v4.7.0, v4.8.2, v4.9 Beta) with synthetic telemetry correlation analytics (e.g. isolating v4.7.0 mesh socket packet degradation) and batch OTA firmware deployment campaigns.
7. **Spare-Parts Supply Chain & Sustainment**: Automated identification of stockouts immobilizing vehicles (e.g. zero-stock `COMM-MOD-V3` blocking `MRD-014` and `MRD-027`), with one-click shipment receipt and purchase order replenishment.
8. **Gemini Readiness Intelligence**: Server-side AI intelligence engine powered by Gemini 3.7 Flash generating 0600Z executive morning briefs and answering conversational operational readiness inquiries.

It does **not** contain or interface with real-world defense systems, classified information, weapons control, targeting, kinetic firing, or offensive guidance capabilities.
